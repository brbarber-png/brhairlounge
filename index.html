OurStory

BR Hair Lounge brings a premium barbering culture to Charlotte. Precision technique, a welcoming atmosphere, and a team that takes pride in every detail.

Here a haircut is more than a haircut. Grab a coffee, put on some music, bring the kids. We welcome walk-ins and appointments, and every client leaves looking and feeling their best.

Meet the barbers

The loungeexperience

Your visit is more than the time in the chair. From the moment you walk in, we want you to relax, feel at home and leave with a look you're proud of.

Coffee & drinks

Grab a coffee or a drink while you wait, with good music in the background.

Family friendly

Kids cuts with patience, and comic books to keep the little ones entertained.

Detail in every cut

We listen first. Eyebrows come free with every haircut, and beards get a hot towel finish.
